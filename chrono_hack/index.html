01About us

When the AI rewrites the past.

Set in a future where an artificial intelligence system has destabilized the timeline, Chrono Hack challenges you to investigate the incident, recover critical information, reconstruct corrupted historical records and build a software solution to prevent reality from collapsing.

Chrono Hack is a three-day, team-based hackathon hosted at ESI from 18 to 20 March 2027. It welcomes 120 students passionate about computer science, programming, cybersecurity, data analysis, algorithms and software development. The whole experience runs on a custom-built interactive website that acts as the event’s virtual time machine, with the challenges, datasets and stages in one place and a way to track your progress.

  1. I

    Investigate

    Recover data. Spot inconsistencies.

  2. II

    Reconstruct

    Cross-check evidence and rebuild a coherent, verified timeline.

  3. III

    Build

    Turn your findings into a working application.

02The stages

Three stages. Three timelines.

Each stage represents a different point in the past or an alternate, corrupted timeline. What you recover in one stage becomes essential to the next.

Day 1 · 18 march

Information recovery & timeline reconstruction

You use the time machine to travel back to the moment of the experiment that caused the crisis. The system has locked the research archives and altered historical records, creating multiple versions of the same timeline. Solve security challenges, recover encrypted information and analyze corrupted datasets.

I
Day 2 · 19 march

Resolution and development

You return to the future, where the timeline is close to complete collapse. Combine the information you recovered and develop an application that addresses a defined objective, which may be proposed by our sponsors.

II
Day 3 · 20 march

Final presentation & evaluation

Each team presents its application to a jury: the problem it addressed, a demo of the solution, and how it used the recovered information.

III
Success

Fix the past

The timeline is restored before it collapses completely.

Failure

The world collapses

The timeline can’t be saved and the world collapses completely.

03FAQ

Got questions? Our answers.

Everything you need to know before the clock starts again.

What is Chrono Hack?

A three-day, team-based hackathon combining technical challenges, data analysis, algorithms and software development in a time-travel storyline.

How does the event work?

Over three days you move through the three stages: recover information, build an application, then present it to a jury.

What skills do I need?

An interest in programming, cybersecurity, data analysis, algorithms or software development. Teams are stronger when skills are mixed.

What do we build on Day 3?

Day 3 is the final presentation: each team demos its application to a jury and explains how it used the recovered information.

What if we fail?

If the timeline can’t be saved, the world collapses completely. That is the storyline, so do your best to fix the past.

Who can join, and how do I register?

The event welcomes 120 students passionate about computer science. Use the Register button to sign up.
